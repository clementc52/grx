How the calls work

Sources. Live flyer and online prices come from Flipp's public search, for every flyer serving the postal areas inside your radius. Daily shelf prices since February 2024 for Loblaws, No Frills, Metro, Walmart, Voila, T&T, Galleria and Save-On-Foods come from Project Hammer (projecthammer.org / jacobfilipp.com). Official monthly averages come from Statistics Canada table 18-10-0245-01, built from retailers' checkout data. Prices are compared per kilogram, litre, egg or roll, so pack sizes don't fool you.

Sale odds. For each chain and item we find past sale episodes (price at least 7% under its trailing regular price, or below the printed "was" price), measure the gaps between them, and fit a Kaplan–Meier survival curve, counting the time since the last sale as a censored observation. Items with few sales borrow from their aisle. "WAIT" means a chain has at least a 50% chance of a sale within 14 days that would save 8% or more. "STOCK UP" means today's best price is at its 90-day low; the quantity covers the expected gap to the next sale, capped by shelf life.

Fine print. Prices marked ~ had no size printed in the flyer and assume the standard pack (a dozen eggs). Flyer prices can differ by store and may need a loyalty card. Always check the shelf tag. Not affiliated with any retailer or with Flipp.
