Where every number comes from

Flyer and online prices: Flipp's public flyer search, for every flyer serving the postal areas inside your radius, checked hourly (every 6 hours for the whole radius, hourly on Thursday nights when flyers change). Online prices are Walmart.ca only. Everyday shelf prices: Project Hammer's daily record of the store-pickup prices at Toronto stores of No Frills, Loblaws, Metro, Walmart, Sobeys/Voilà, T&T, Galleria and Save-On-Foods, reloaded within the hour of each daily update. In-store prices: shopper receipts and shelf-tag photos from Open Prices (Open Food Facts, ODbL); add yours. Official average: Statistics Canada table 18-10-0245-01.

How a price gets on this page. It must match the grocery's definition (for example, large eggs, not medium, liquid or boiled), print a clear size (listings with size ranges or several products at different sizes are skipped, and sizes are never assumed), fall within a sanity range for that grocery, and agree with the other sources: a price far from every other price for the same item is treated as a reading error and hidden. Prices are compared per kilogram, litre or egg.

Limits. Flyer prices can need a loyalty card or a multi-buy, shown next to the price. Shelf prices are a day old at most. Distances are to the postal area a flyer serves, not to the store's door. Not affiliated with any retailer or with Flipp. Always check the shelf tag.
